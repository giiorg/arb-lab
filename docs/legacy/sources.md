## Uncertainty flags

1. **Live point-in-time funding values for May 9, 2026 not retrievable.** Coinglass not exposed in search; The Block returned 403. The May 9, 2026 _level_ is reported as "near zero / mildly negative" based on May 7, 2026 Coindesk reporting and the 46-day negative streak ending mid-April. Specific intraday values should be cross-checked on Coinglass directly.

2. **Per-market caps and floors** for Binance, OKX, and Backpack vary by symbol and are updated by exchange announcement; the values quoted (±2%, ±0.05%, ±0.75 × MM rate) are defaults — verify per pair via the contract spec or API.

3. **OKX 2025 formula change rollout** is documented as a 3-batch deployment (April 10/17/24, 2025); the formula above reflects the post-rollout state, but legacy historical data uses the pre-2025 formula.

4. **dYdX v4 cross-market interest rate** is currently 0% by default in the open-source code, but a governance vote could activate a nonzero value at any time.

5. **Drift Protocol funding** is described in v2 docs as "lazy hourly" — exact behavior under no-trade conditions depends on keeper-bot economics and may have changed; the base formula $\frac{1}{24} \cdot (M-O)/O$ is the documented core.

6. **GMX v2 funding factor / borrowing factor values** are governance-set per market and frequently retuned. The functional forms are stable; the constants are not.

7. **Hyperliquid HIP-3 perms** use a different premium formula than standard markets — this is verified but the exact list of HIP-3-eligible markets evolves.

8. **sUSDe APY varies materially by methodology** (3.72% protocol-level on DefiLlama vs. 9.4% trailing-7d sUSDe stake APY). Cross-check both before quoting any single number.

9. **Ethena TVL also varies by source** ($3.96B DefiLlama vs. $6.66B aggregate). Methodology differs (on-chain aggregate vs. protocol fee/revenue page).

10. **Attribution of funding-rate compression to Ethena specifically** is plausible but not cleanly proven. Bull-market exhaustion + retail leverage shake-out are confounding factors.

11. **Bybit / OKX insurance fund sizes** are not transparently disclosed in 2026.

12. **Wash-sale extension to crypto** is possible 2026-27 but not law as of May 2026.

13. **Some retail-arbitrage return claims** (15-30%, 100%+ on alts) come from Twitter/Medium operators whose books are not audited; treat as anecdotal.

14. **Specific firm tech stacks** (Wintermute, Cumberland, GSR, B2C2): "publicly known" or "likely" reflect that these firms do not publish full architecture documents. Statements about their stacks come from talks, blog posts, and public hires; direct verification is not possible.

15. **GitHub star counts and last-push dates** are accurate as of May 9, 2026 but evolve continuously.
